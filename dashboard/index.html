How values work

TVL × peer multiple × weekly growth factor × maturity factor. We take medians weighted by TVL similarity of circulating market cap divided by TVL, then blend category and broad peers in log space. Stronger category support gets more weight.

Growth is capped at −35% and +50%, then square-root scaled. Maturity rises from 0.75× to 1× over three years using DefiLlama tracking age. Missing growth is neutral and missing age uses the minimum. The middle 50% of peer multiples shows spread, not launch odds.
